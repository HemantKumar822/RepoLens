# Bundled city models

These low-poly 3D models are from [Kit Cidade Low Poly](https://github.com/kayquer/kit-cidade-lowpoly) by kayquer. The upstream repository dedicates its models to the public domain under CC0 1.0; the full license text is in `CC0-1.0.txt`. Attribution is not required, but the source is recorded here. Models are bundled locally so the city does not fetch third-party assets at runtime.

The filenames in this folder are friendly aliases for the selected upstream models. They are used as reusable city buildings, park trees, street furniture, a moving taxi, and the central plaza monument. Roads, lane markings, crossings, and the coastline are generated locally from the map layout.
